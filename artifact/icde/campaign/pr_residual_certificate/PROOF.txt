Let a be the exact rational value of binary64(.85), b the exact rational value of binary64(.15), and d=1-a. Do not assume a+b=1 in exact arithmetic. For the exact positive weighted transition P, including uniform redistribution of dangling mass, ||P^T||_1=1. F(x)=b/n*1+a*P^T*x has a unique fixed point p and ||x-p||_1 <= ||x-F(x)||_1/d.

Domain checks inspect the entire CSR: square FP64; 0<n<2^31, 0<=m<2^31; valid monotone offsets and vertex endpoints; every weight is a finite integer1..16. A row sum is an integer <=16m<2^35, so its FP64 reduction is exact. IEEE binary64 round-to-nearest is checked. Positive reciprocal, multiply, sparse accumulation, dangling reduction/division, addition, damping and teleport operations have no cancellation. u=2^-53 and gamma(k)=ku/(1-ku). The standard product bound follows directly by multiplying individual factors (1+delta_i), |delta_i|<=u: their absolute product error is <=gamma(k). A sparse contribution has at most m reductions and constant operations; a dangling contribution has at most n reductions and constant operations. k=m+n+32 conservatively covers either complete path. Teleport-only error uses gamma(32). Therefore for M=||x||_1:
  ||flF(x)-F(x)||_1 <= E(M) = a*gamma(k)*M+b*gamma(32)+tau.
Here tau=64(m+n+1)*2^-1022 conservatively covers all underflow/flush-to-zero losses, including integer weight scaling. No overflow is possible in the supported nonnegative mass/weight domain.
